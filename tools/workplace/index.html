1. Data Ingestion & Status Mapping

  • Template Aggregation: Combines facilities from CMRT, EMRT, AMRT, and RMI Public List by Facility ID (CID).
  • DD Audit Matching: Matches against official Conformant & Active lists.
  • CAHRA Identification: Automatically matches facility country against EU Regulation (EU) 2017/821 and US Dodd-Frank Act 1502 standards.
  • User-defined Smelters of Concern: Automatically matches facilities manually defined by the user.
  • Revision Tracking: Preserves delisted facilities and flags them as "Removed".
  • In-depth Verification: Filters across all columns including Operation, Level, and CAHRA Basis.
