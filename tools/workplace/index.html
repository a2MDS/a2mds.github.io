📖 Smelter Log User Manual

1. Data Ingestion & Status Mapping

  • Template Aggregation: Combines facilities from CMRT, EMRT, AMRT, and RMI Public List by Facility ID (CID).
  • CAHRA Identification: Automatically matches facility country against EU Regulation (EU) 2017/821 and US Dodd-Frank Act 1502 standards.
  • RMAP Audit Matching: Matches against official Conformant & Active lists.
  • Revision Tracking: Preserves delisted facilities as Revision (Removed).

2. Cloud Sync & Multi-Column Filtering

  • Multi-column filtering across all columns including Operation, Level, and CAHRA Basis.
  • Instant synchronization with Google Drive backup options.
