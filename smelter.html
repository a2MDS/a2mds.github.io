📖 Detailed Guide to Smelter Consolidation

Smelter Pulse cross-references 6 RMI XML files to maintain supply chain audit transparency:

Step 1. Base Facility Extraction (CMRT, EMRT, AMRT)

  • CMRT, EMRT, AMRT standard list XML files are parsed.
  • Extracts core baseline metadata: Metal, Reference, Standard Smelter Name, Country, CID, City, and State.

Step 2. RMAP Conformant & Audit Enrichment

  • Parses Conformant Smelters XML and matches by CID.
  • Assigns RMAP Status = 'Conformant' and captures audit details: Last audit date / Cycle / Reaudit status.

Step 3. Active Smelters Cross-Referencing

  • Parses Active Smelters XML. Facilities undergoing assessment are marked with RMAP Status = 'Active'.
  • Facilities not listed in conformant or active are classified as Standard ('-').

Step 4. Revision History & Removed Smelter Preservation

  • Parses Revision History XML. Preserves historic smelters that existed previously but are now omitted as Type = 'REVISION' & RMAP Status = 'Removed'.
